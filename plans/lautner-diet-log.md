## How to Use in PepTrack

1. Open the **Diet** tab each morning — it shows today's six meals with times.
2. Tap a meal when eaten to check it off. Checked meals turn emerald.
3. If you swap a food, tap **Swap** on that meal, enter what you ate and its macros. The daily macro bar recalculates automatically.
4. The carry-bag snack is optional between meals — log it if you use it.
5. End of day: macro bar must land within 10 g protein / 10 g fat / 20 g carbs of target, or the day flags amber.

## Cheat / Refeed Policy

One cheat day risks dropping a few pounds. If you take one:
- Treat it as a **planned refeed**, not a free-for-all
- Log it in the swap table with actual macros eaten
- Resume normal meals at the next scheduled time — do not "make up" by skipping
- Expect 1–3 lbs water weight fluctuation the following 48 hours; judge progress on the 7-day weekly average, not the next morning
