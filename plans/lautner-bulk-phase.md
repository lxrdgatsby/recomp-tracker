# Lautner Bulk Phase (Eclipse Prep)

Source: Jordan Yuam's published program for Taylor Lautner's New Moon/Eclipse transformation.
Adapted for Gatsby at 156 lbs targeting 186 lbs lean mass.

---

## Overview

- **Frequency:** 5 days/week, 2 hours/day
- **Rest:** Day off every 3rd day, never train past 5 PM
- **Warm-up:** Stretches + push-ups or air squats before every session
- **Goal weight:** 186 lbs (add 30 lbs of muscle from 156)
- **Start surplus:** ~3,200 kcal/day, add 200 kcal/week if no gain
- **Protein:** 186 g/day | **Fat:** 93 g/day | **Carbs:** remainder

---

## Yuam's Three Rules (baked into every session)

1. **Taste the weight** — load ~40% above your normal 10-rep max, only perform the lowering (eccentric) half with a spotter. 2–3 sets of 5, every other week.
2. **Vary volume** — rotate between 3 sets of 8–10 and 4 sets of 15 across weeks.
3. **Cap cardio** — never exceed 20 minutes; it burns the surplus you're trying to keep.

## Weekly Split

### Monday — Bench & Assistance
- Barbell bench press: 5×5 heavy
- Close-grip tricep press: 4×15
- Dumbbell incline bench: 3×8–10
- Dips: 4×15
- Dumbbell overhead tricep extension: 3×8–10
- Incline chest flys: 4×15 light

### Tuesday — Deadlift & Assistance
- Deadlift: 5×5
- Cable rows: 4×15
- Lat pulldowns: 3×8–10
- T-bar rows: 4×15
- Dumbbell curls: 3×8–10
- Preacher curls: 4×15

### Wednesday — Squats & Assistance
- Back squat: 5×5
- Weighted lunges: 4×15
- Leg press: 3×8–10
- Box jumps: 4×15
- Calf raises: 3×8–10
- Hamstring curls: 4×15

### Thursday — Shoulders
- Military press: 5×5
- Front raises: 4×15
- Shrugs: 3×8–10
- Face pulls: 4×15
- Arnold press: 3×8–10
- Dumbbell shrugs: 4×15

### Friday — Arms & Core Finisher
- Superset pairs back-to-back, no rest between exercises

### Abs (3 non-consecutive days)
- Swiss ball pikes: 3–4×8–12
- Reverse crunches: 3×15
- Swiss ball leg curls: 3–4×10–12
- Hanging leg raises: hold 7–10 sec at top
- Prone cobra: 3×60 sec

## Diet — 6 Meals, Nothing More Than 2 Hours Apart

**Daily targets (start ~3,200 kcal, scale up):**
- Calories: 3,200 (ramp +200/wk if stalled)
- Protein: 186 g
- Fat: 93 g
- Carbs: ~330 g

### Meal Schedule

1. **6:00 AM — Protein shake** (before fully awake)
2. **Breakfast:** 4-egg omelet, yogurt, whole wheat toast, orange juice
3. **Mid-morning snack:** nuts, strawberries, blueberries
4. **Lunch:** roasted chicken, rice, green beans, fish
5. **Post-workout:** protein bar, banana, raspberries
6. **Dinner:** salmon, sweet potato, mixed vegetables, green tea

### Between-Meal Carry Bag
- Beef patties
- Raw almonds
- Sweet potatoes

**Rules:** Lean meats and veggies only. No junk food. One cheat day risks dropping a few pounds — treat it as a planned refeed, not a free-for-all.

---

## Progression Notes

- Log every session in PepTrack with weight × reps per set
- When you hit the top of a rep range on all sets, increase load 5 lbs next session
- Weigh in daily, average weekly; if weekly average is flat for 7 days, add 200 kcal
- Track photos weekly (front/side/back) to confirm muscle gain vs fat gain
- Retatrutide: consider tapering or pausing during bulk — the drug's appetite suppression fights the surplus
