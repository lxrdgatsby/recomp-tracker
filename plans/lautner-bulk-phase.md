# Lautner Bulk Phase (Eclipse Prep)

Source: Jordan Yuam's published program for Taylor Lautner's New Moon/Eclipse transformation.
Adapted for Gatsby at 156 lbs targeting 186 lbs lean mass.
Current position: Week 11 of 90-day recomp (Nov 2026). Bulk continues past day 90.

---

## Overview

- **Frequency:** 5 days/week, ~75 min/session (12:45 PM – 2:00 PM)
- **Rest:** Day off every 3rd day, never train past 5 PM
- **Warm-up:** Stretches + push-ups or air squats before every session
- **Goal weight:** 186 lbs (add 30 lbs of muscle from 156)
- **Start surplus:** ~3,200 kcal/day, add 200 kcal/week if no gain
- **Protein:** 186 g/day | **Fat:** 93 g/day | **Carbs:** remainder

**Prescribed window: 12:45 PM – ~2:00 PM**, immediately before the 2:00 PM post-workout meal (protein bar, banana, raspberries), which is eaten right after lifting.

- Yuam's structure is 50 min lifting + 15 min abs + 10 min stretching = ~75 min total. The treadmill warm-up runs at the front, so the session ends right around 2:00 PM.
- Training lands right after the 12:00 PM lunch and keeps the 6:00 AM – 10:00 AM eating window (shake, breakfast, mid-morning snack) clear of any session.
- Well inside the "never train past 5 PM" rule.

## Dedicated Cardio Protocol (Dec 1 bulk start)

**20 minutes, 12% incline, 3 mph — AFTER lifting and stretching, never before.**

- **Days:** Monday, Wednesday, Friday (bench, squat, arms days)
- **Timing:** ~2:15 PM, after the 10-min stretch, right before the 2:00 PM-shifted post-workout meal window
- **Settings:** 12% incline, 3.0 mph, 20:00 duration
- **Why this placement:** Research (umbrella review of 17 meta-analyses) shows lifting first preserves strength output; cardio before lifting measurably reduces same-session strength. Six-plus hours separation is ideal but unrealistic with this meal schedule, so post-lift is the next best slot.
- **Muscle preservation:** 12% incline walking at 3 mph has almost no eccentric load, fires glutes 30–50% harder than flat walking, and burns ~250–300 kcal for a 155–180 lb person. Low catabolic potential compared to running.
- **Retatrutide note:** The drug suppresses appetite, so every cardio calorie must be consciously replaced. This is why the 20-min cap matters more now than it did for Lautner at 17 eating 3,600 on autopilot. If strength slips on the same loads, cut to 2 days/week or drop incline to 8%.
- **Tracking:** Weekly average weight must climb ~1 lb/week by week 3 of the bulk or the surplus — not the cardio — is the problem. Add 200 kcal if flat 7 days.
- **Rest days:** Optional, any time, no incline requirement.

## Yuam's Three Rules (baked into every session)

1. **Taste the weight** — load ~40% above your normal 10-rep max, only perform the lowering (eccentric) half with a spotter. 2–3 sets of 5, every other week.
2. **Vary volume** — rotate between 3 sets of 8–10 and 4 sets of 15 across weeks.
3. **Cap cardio** — never exceed 20 minutes; it burns the surplus you're trying to keep.

| Weeks | Block | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| 11–12 | Base solidification | Keep 3×8–10 volume. Log all working weights. No new maxes. |

## Progression Timeline (starts Week 11, maps Lautner's full Eclipse arc)

Lautner went 140→170 lbs over roughly 12 months. Your 156→186 is the same 30-lb arc, so the blocks scale 1:1.

### Phase 1 — Base Building (Weeks 11–14)
- All main lifts at moderate loads, 3×8–10 volume
- No eccentric "taste the weight" sessions yet
- Cardio: 20 min @ 12% incline, 3 mph, 3x/week (Mon/Wed/Fri), after stretching
- Target: +1 lb/week on weekly average
- Food swaps logged in diet tab if anything changes from the template

### Phase 2 — Volume Rotation Begins (Weeks 15–18)
- Rotate to 4×15 on assistance work (rows, curls, raises, flys)
- Main lifts stay 5×5
- Introduce taste-the-weight eccentrics on ONE lift per week (rotate: bench → deadlift → squat → military)
- Cardio capped at 20 min
- If weekly average is flat 7 days: +200 kcal

### Phase 3 — Eccentric Emphasis (Weeks 19–22)
- Taste-the-weight on two lifts per week
- Volume peaks: 4×15 everywhere assistance work allows
- Deload week 22: drop all loads 40%, keep movement patterns
- Photos: front/side/back weekly — confirm muscle vs fat

### Phase 4 — Strength Peak (Weeks 23–26)
- Back to 3×8–10 on assistance, heavier 5×5 on mains
- Eccentrics on all four main lifts, every other week
- Cardio: 10 min max or zero
- Expected weight: ~168–172 lbs

### Phase 5 — Second Cycle (Weeks 27–30)
- Restart volume rotation at higher baselines (everything +10–15 lbs from Phase 1 numbers)
- New food swaps allowed only if logged with macro-equivalent replacement
- +200 kcal if stalled

### Phase 6 — Final Push (Weeks 31–34)
- Peak loads, minimal assistance volume
- Taste-the-weight weekly on rotation
- Expected weight: ~178–182 lbs
- Final deload week 34

### Phase 7 — Close to Target (Weeks 35–38)
- Hold at 186 target, tighten to maintenance surplus
- Taper Retatrutide fully if not already off
- Shift to recomposition: keep protein 186 g, drop to ~2,800 kcal to lean out without losing the bulk

### Beyond Week 38
- Maintenance: 186 g protein, ~2,600–2,800 kcal, 4 days/week training
- Keep the diet log running — food swaps still tracked

- Log every session in PepTrack with weight × reps per set

- Track photos weekly (front/side/back) to confirm muscle gain vs fat gain
